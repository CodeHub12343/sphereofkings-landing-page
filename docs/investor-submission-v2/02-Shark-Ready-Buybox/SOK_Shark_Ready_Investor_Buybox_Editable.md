## ZONE 2 — 10-BEAT BUYBOX

### 02 · Problem

- Flat boards and single-use puzzles get played once and shelved.
- Legacy toy and puzzle production cannot economically make one-off customized designs.
- Independent artists and brands have no clean path from a design to a shelf-ready physical product.
- Collectors want premium, numbered limited editions; mass-market manufacturers will not run short batches.
- Short-run polymer and metal industrial work is underserved by both bureau shops and in-house capacity.

### 03 · Solution

- Flat, play-once boards → **one spherical hardware platform running seven distinct game and puzzle experiences** (Royal Checkers · Imperial Kings · Pre-designed Puzzle · Custom Upload Puzzle · DIY Paint Puzzle · Paintable Royal Checkers · Paintable Imperial Kings).
- No cost-effective customization → **customer-uploaded and paintable editions produced on an additive print farm**.
- No path for creators → **creator marketplace with a 50/50 net-profit royalty model**.
- No short-run premium production → **numbered limited editions, including future metal pieces**.
- Short-run industrial work underserved → **same polymer and metal capacity sold as contract manufacturing**.

### 04 · Why now

- Prosumer additive hardware (Bambu Lab class) has brought per-unit polymer printing cost into range for consumer products.
- Kickstarter remains the most efficient launch channel for distinctive consumer hardware — SOK's campaign is scheduled for **November 19, 2026**.
- The creator economy has normalized royalty-based licensing of independent designs.
- Metal additive manufacturing has matured enough that short-run specialty parts are commercially addressable.
