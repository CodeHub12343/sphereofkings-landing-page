## 8 · One-page design discipline

Design decisions made to keep the document on exactly one landscape page:

- Zone 1 (identity + hook) sits in a single 38 mm navy band; the hook line occupies the shared row to reclaim vertical space usually lost to a full header strip.
- Zone 2 uses a 2×5 grid of beats at approximately 24 mm each; type sizes are set at the lower edge of the master-prompt range (8.5–9 pt body, 7.5 pt labels) but held above the master-prompt minimum.
- Beat 10 (the ask) inverts the color scheme to navy-on-gold inside the grid — one visual anchor replaces the dedicated "Zone 3 ask panel" to save a full row.
- Zone 3 collapses to a single two-column footer strip at ~10 mm, holding the disclaimer, DD-folder placeholder, and the document identifier.
- All text sits inside the 11 mm safe margin.

If any of the content needs to grow (e.g. updated Founder beat), one of the current bullets must contract by the same amount. The one-page constraint is non-negotiable per the master prompt.
