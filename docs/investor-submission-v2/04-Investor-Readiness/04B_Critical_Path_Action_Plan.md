The CSV is the authoritative list — 41 actions grouped into five phases. Open in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets and filter by Phase, Priority, or Owner.

## Phases

### Immediate (Week 1–3)

Document and evidence collection. The actions James must start in the first week. Includes compiling the contractor list, requesting IP assignments, downloading bank statements, standing up the DD folder, and formally requesting the Jellop / PostcardMania / IP-firm evidence.

### Short term (Weeks 2–10)

Corporate, IP, financial, and commercial verification. Entity formation, EIN, operating agreement, cap table, securities-counsel engagement, FTO memo, patent filings, trademark filings, bottom-up financial model, toy-safety testing, insurance, privacy / ToS review, and the Kickstarter launch itself (2026-11-19).

### Medium term (Months 2–9)

Operational, manufacturing, and market validation. BOM + cycle time validation, equipment RFQs (three per class), three-site shortlist, first industrial pilot customer, creator-marketplace template, CFO + CTO hires, board appointment.

### Pre-submission (final week before investor circulation)

Final review and investor data-room preparation. All placeholders replaced, PDFs re-rendered, independent accountant review, securities-counsel review, all approval blocks signed.

### Post-submission (ongoing)

Investor follow-up triage, population of 07-Supporting-Evidence/ as each document arrives, and ongoing updates to the Source & Assumption Register and Readiness Checklist.

## How this plan is governed

- No action in **Pre-submission** starts until every P0 action in **Immediate** and **Short term** is complete.
- Actions marked `IP counsel`, `Securities counsel`, `Corporate counsel`, `Financial consultant`, `Insurance broker`, `Accountant`, or `Test lab` cannot be completed by the document-preparation team.
- Actions owned by `Founder` are decisions or disclosures only James can make. See `06C_Management_Decisions_Required.md` for the discrete decisions.
- Each completed action updates the matching Status in the relevant register (03B–03K) and the Source & Assumption Register (05).
