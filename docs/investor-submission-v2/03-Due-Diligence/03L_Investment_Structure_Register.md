# 03L · Investment Structure & Capital Deployment

**Status of the offering as of 2026-11-04:** **Proposed.** No commitment letter, term sheet, or signed investment agreement exists. Investment terms, valuation, ownership, security type, and timing remain subject to negotiation, documentation, professional review, and the execution of binding definitive agreements.

## 1 · Proposed capital-raising target

- **Amount:** USD $100M (proposed)
- **Structure:** Staged, milestone-gated. Later-stage tranches are released only when prior-stage deliverables are documented and verified — not on a calendar.
- **Anticipated security type:** Convertible preferred shares with negotiated protective provisions, board rights, and information rights appropriate to each stage. Equipment-finance debt may supplement equity where it reduces dilution without creating excessive fixed obligations.

## 4 · Milestone-gated release conditions

Each release is contractually conditioned on documented evidence of:

- Previous-stage operational deliverables.
- Audited or management-provided financial statements (as agreed).
- Updated risk register.
- Independent verification where required by the agreement.

The Company proposes this structure to investors as a feature (capital discipline), not a constraint.

## 5 · Governance considerations

- Investor board seat anticipated at Stage 1.
- Independent director by Stage 2.
- Second investor seat at Stage 3.
- Independent chair optional by Stage 4.
- Protective provisions on capex above threshold, new debt, M&A, charter changes, related-party transactions.
- Information rights: monthly operating KPIs, quarterly unaudited financials, annual audited financials by Stage 2.

## 6 · Potential strategic investors

The Company is open to investors with manufacturing, distribution, technology, or licensing expertise. No strategic investor is identified or committed as of this document.

## 7 · Potential equipment financing

Equipment debt may be appropriate for high-ticket production lines (e.g. injection molding, CNC, metal additive cells) once creditworthiness is established. Terms, rates, and collateral unknown.
