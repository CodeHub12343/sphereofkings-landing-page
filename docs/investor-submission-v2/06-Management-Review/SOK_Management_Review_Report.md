- **Ownership math.** The $100M for 25% at a $300M pre-money is **a modeling convention, not an offer or term sheet**. Every mention of it now carries that qualification.
- **ROI assumptions.** All investor-outcome rows now carry a footer: pre-dilution, pre-tax, pre-transaction-cost, no preferred-share adjustments.
- **Third-party imagery removed.** The earlier draft referred to Forgely, "3D Printing Industry", and Innovise IT as visual stand-ins. v2.0 uses only Sphere of Kings' own 36 approved product images and schematic diagrams prepared for the document — no implied endorsement.
