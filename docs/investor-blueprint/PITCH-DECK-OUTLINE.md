## Design rules for the deck

- Use **only** figures and claims that appear in the Blueprint — no new numbers in the deck.
- Every financial slide carries a one-line footer: *Illustrative target; see Investor Master Blueprint for assumptions.*
- Cover and section-divider slides use dark navy `#101827` with a restrained gold rule under the title.
- Body slides use the warm-white background `#F8F9FB` with charcoal `#273142` body text.
- Charts use the Blueprint's data; brand gold is accent only, not fill-every-bar.
- Product photography pulls from `nextjs/public/images/products/` (36 approved images). No stock imagery.

---

## Deliverable ownership

- Keep the deck under one 16:9 widescreen master, 14 slides, under 10MB PDF.
- Export as both `.pdf` and the editable source once the Blueprint figures are confirmed.
- Do **not** circulate the deck without the Blueprint and Source Register.
