## Highest-priority blockers before external submission

1. **Document Shark Ready / Rabbit Capital status** (A1 in Source Register). The Blueprint cannot represent the $100M as "secured" or imply a Rabbit Capital commitment.
2. **Current capitalization table** (F.1). Investors will ask within the first 10 minutes.
3. **IP ownership confirmation** (G.1). Any ambiguity here is fatal to a $100M raise.
4. **Bottom-up financial model** (A). The current revenue/EBITDA figures are ambitions; investors will not accept top-down targets at this scale.
5. **Equipment quotations for Stage 2 only** (B). Stage 1–2 deployment plan should be supported by real quotations even if later stages are planning estimates.
6. **Facility feasibility study for an initial smaller pilot facility** (C), not a 150K sq. ft. flagship.
