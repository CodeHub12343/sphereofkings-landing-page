# Non-Digital Game Modes — Phase 0 Confirmation

> Status: **owner-approved** (Phase 0 sign-off, 2026-10-19).

The seven-in-one lineup includes five modes that are **physical-product only** at launch. They are intentionally excluded from the digital gaming platform's initial roadmap. This document records which, why, and what their future digital status could be.

---

## Physical-only at launch (not in digital MVP)

### 1. Spherical Puzzle™
- **Digital status**: deferred to Phase 2 of the digital platform (official puzzles only) and Phase 2+ (custom-upload).
- **Reason for deferral**: puzzle gameplay is a different interaction model than board games (drag-drop + rotation) and does not share engine code with Royal Checkers / Imperial Kings. Needs its own build-out.

### 2. DIY Paintable Experience™
- **Digital status**: addressed by the sphere-painting feature in Phase 7 of the digital platform (not a game mode but a creativity tool with a "preview on sphere" surface).
- **Reason**: fundamentally a physical experience; the digital analogue is the painter + 3D preview, not a game.

### 3. Custom Sphere Upload™
- **Digital status**: Phase 2+ as part of the custom-image puzzle pipeline.
- **Reason**: already slated for digital; the physical and digital versions share the "turn an image into a puzzle" concept but ship from different pipelines.

### 4. Expanded Sphere Challenges™
- **Digital status**: **not planned for the digital platform**. Specifications are proposed; the mode will remain a physical component bundle sold through the shop.
- **Reason**: owner has not finalised Expanded Challenges rules; the concept is additional physical puzzles and strategy prompts rather than a new game engine.
- **Revisit**: if demand emerges post-launch, could be bundled into Phase 9 as a new puzzle category.

### 5. Creator's Edition™
- **Digital status**: **not planned for the digital platform**.
- **Reason**: Creator's Edition is explicitly a physical "build it yourself" product. The digital analogue is already covered by the sphere-painting + custom-puzzle features in Phases 2 + 7.

## Owner note (2026-10-19)
The digital platform does not have to be a 1:1 mirror of the seven physical modes. The physical product is a gift, a collectible, and a craft experience; the digital platform is a competitive game surface. Overlap is deliberate but not exhaustive.

If any of the "Not planned" modes should be revisited for digital release later, open a new entry in the owner decision log of the platform roadmap.
